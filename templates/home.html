Got Questions?

Dukaandaron Ke Common Sawaal

SmartBill use karne se pehle jo sawaal aapke dimaag me aa sakte hain, unke clear answers:

Kya mujhe digital bill share karne ke liye koi SMS pack ya gateway recharge karwana hoga?
Bilkul nahi! SmartBill dynamic QR code technology use karta hai. Customer aapki screen ya counter stand par QR code scan karta hai aur bill unke phone ke browser me turant khul jata hai. Isme dukaandar ko 1 rupaye ka bhi SMS charge nahi lagta — yeh 100% free hai.
Agar kisi customer ko paper bill hi chahiye, toh kya mai printer se print nikal sakta hu?
Haan, bilkul! SmartBill paperless-first hai lekin physical thermal printers (58mm aur 80mm) ke sath fully compatible hai. Har bill ke sath 1-click 'Print Invoice' button hota hai jisse turant thermal receipt nikal aati hai.
Customer ko QR code scan karne ke liye koi special application download karni padegi?
Nahi! Customer apne phone ke normal camera, Google Lens, Google Pay, Paytm, PhonePe ya WhatsApp me se kisi se bhi QR scan kar sakta hai. Bill seedha unke phone ke Chrome ya Safari browser me beautifully render hota hai bina kisi app install kiye.
Kya mai apne mobile phone ya tablet se bhi billing kar sakta hu, ya laptop compulsory hai?
Aap kisi bhi device par chala sakte hain! SmartBill fully responsive web-based platform hai. Aap chahein toh apne Android/iPhone mobile phone se counter par ghoom-ghoom kar bill banayein, ya counter tablet ya desktop PC par chalayein.
Mera shop data, inventory aur sales records kitna safe aur private hain?
Aapka saara data encrypted database me safely store hota hai. Har shop ka isolated database partition hota hai aur multi-user role management ke through cashiers aapki sensitive profit settings nahi dekh sakte.
Kya mai apne staff ya cashier ke liye alag login bana sakta hu?
Ji haan! SmartBill me Role-Based Access Control hai. Aap staff ke liye Cashier role de sakte hain jisse wo sirf bill bana sakein aur inventory dekh sakein, jabki pricing change karna aur total shop reports dekhna sirf Owner ke paas rehta hai.
